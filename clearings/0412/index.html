Nine minutes' walk. Eighteen metres above the sea, facing 271°, which means the last hour of light lands on the long wall and stays there until it goes.

Nine hundred and forty square metres of clearing. Three hundred and ten of it roofed: one long room, one wing set back behind the trees the plan had to work around, and a deck that stops where the roots start.

The pool is fourteen metres. It was drawn at twelve until a Yang Na root made the argument.

The floor is teak on sand-set stone and it is cool until about eleven.
