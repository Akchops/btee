Ko Lamphu — surveyed 2004–2008 1,412 trees · 24 clearings Every mark is one tagged tree. The gaps are the villas.

Ko Lamphu is forest to the waterline. The only thing above the trees is weather.

Four years before she drew anything, Kesorn Ampai walked the island and tagged every tree above forty centimetres in girth.

One thousand four hundred and twelve.

And the places where nothing was growing.

Twenty-four clearings

She wrote three conditions and never wrote a fourth.

  1. I

    Nothing rises above the canopy.

  2. II

    Nothing is built where a mature tree stands.

  3. III

    No light above the knee after dark.

The second rule is the expensive one. It means no villa was ever placed. Each was found, and each is a different shape, because each clearing is a different shape.

Twenty-four is not a number we chose.

The path climbs for four minutes. Somewhere in the middle of it the temperature drops, and that is the first thing the island does to you.

Nine minutes' walk. Eighteen metres above the sea, facing 271°, which means the last hour of light lands on the long wall and stays there until it goes.

Nine hundred and forty square metres of clearing. Three hundred and ten of it roofed.

The pool is fourteen metres. It was drawn at twelve until a Yang Na root made the argument.

There are twenty-four plans and none of them is a mirror of another.

The Clearings — all twenty-four

  1. 07:00

    The forest is loudest at seven and again at five. In between it is mostly insects and the sea.

  2. 11:00

    The floor is cool until about eleven. Teak on sand-set stone; it holds the night longer than it ought to. By eleven it has given up and you are in the water.

  3. 13:00Hu Kwang

    Charcoal on the sand from noon. Whatever came in on the long-tail that morning, and not much else. There is no menu; there is a boat.

    Hu Kwang

  4. 15:00Water

    The reef starts eleven metres out and drops to eighteen. You don't need a boat to reach it, which is unusual here.

    Water

  5. 16:00

    Rain comes at four in the wet season and is finished by five. The forest is loud for an hour afterwards and everything smells like it has been opened.

  6. 17:00Takhian

    One room under one tree. Cold water, hot water, and someone who has been doing this for nineteen years.

    Takhian

From the water, the island goes out.

0930 · Chik opens at eight. The tree it's named for opens at ten. The flowers last one night — by six they are on the ground and the whole floor of the forest is white.

There is no light within forty kilometres of here.

You will see more than you expect to.

The ridge is fifty-eight metres, twenty-three minutes' walk. It's the only place on the island where you stand above the canopy — and you're only allowed up here because nothing is built on it.

South of here the island runs on for another kilometre and there is nothing on it. Six clearings were surveyed in 2007 and left where they were.
