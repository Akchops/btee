The South End

South of the ridge the island runs on for another kilometre. Six clearings were surveyed in 2007 and left where they were.

They are the only land on Ko Lamphu that can be owned. What is bought is not a house — none is built — but a site, its survey, and a covenant.

  1. INothing rises above the canopy.
  2. IINothing is built where a mature tree stands.
  3. IIINo light above the knee after dark.
  4. IVThe covenant passes with the land, and cannot be varied by its owner.

Rule IV is the only condition Kesorn Ampai added after the resort opened, and it is the reason the first three still hold.
