What proves what

Manual level buttons in the HUD prove each degraded state renders correctly. They prove nothing about detection.

Only an auto escalation during the stress test proves the watchdog actually detects sustained frame pressure. The report separates the two and never merges them.

Run order

  1. Enable the HUD, open the island.
  2. Scroll the homepage top to bottom at a normal reading pace. Watch p90.
  3. Pause at beat 01 (the survey resolving) and beat 06 (trees becoming stars). Note frame time in each.
  4. Navigate home → /clearings/. Judge the survey-field transition: coherent, or awkward?
  5. Rotate to landscape, repeat 2–4, rotate back.
  6. Hard reload with cache cleared. Watch the hero line for a font jump; read font-swap Δwidth.
  7. Turn Reduce Motion ON in OS settings, reload. Dapple stays and stops; survey renders complete. Turn it off.
  8. On /clearings/, one tap a clearing: it selects and opens its record; the record's link opens the villa.
  9. Enable VoiceOver / TalkBack and swipe through the 24 clearing controls.
  10. Press Stress while scrolling. Watch whether escalations (auto) rises before you see stutter.
  11. Press Build & copy report, paste it back.

Notes

Frame samples persist across navigations in this session — press Reset between scenarios you want measured separately. On plain http over a LAN the clipboard API is blocked; the report textarea is the reliable route, so select and copy it manually.

The fallback face is inferred by width comparison and is approximate. Thresholds shown are hypotheses, not targets — do not tune anything from a single reading.
